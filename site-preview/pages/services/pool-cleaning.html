What's Included in Every Weekly Visit

A Florida pool works hard year-round — sun, rain, pollen, and constant swim season mean the water never gets a break. Our weekly cleaning service keeps your pool swim-ready without you touching a net or a test strip. Every visit covers the same complete checklist, and you get a service report when we're done.

  • Skim the surface, brush walls and steps, and vacuum the pool floor
  • Empty skimmer and pump baskets so water keeps moving at full flow
  • Clean or backwash the filter as the pressure reading requires
  • Full water chemistry test and balancing on every visit
  • Salt cell check on salt water pools — output, scale, and salt level
  • Equipment inspection with photo documentation of anything we find
  • Service report sent after each visit so you know exactly what was done

Water Chemistry Balancing, Not Just Testing

Most pool problems start in the water, not the equipment. Chlorine that swings between too low and too high, pH drifting acidic, alkalinity out of range — these are what turn a clear pool green, etch surfaces, and quietly destroy heaters, salt cells, and seals. That's why chemical balancing is a full step on every visit, not a dip-strip glance.

We test free chlorine, pH, total alkalinity, calcium hardness, cyanuric acid, and salt level where applicable — then adjust on the spot. Balanced water protects your swimmers, your pool surface, and every piece of equipment on the pad. It's the cheapest equipment insurance there is.

Equipment Experts, Not Just Cleaners

Here's what makes our cleaning service different: the technician at your pool works for a licensed pool contractor and knows the equipment pad as well as the water. Every visit doubles as an equipment inspection — pump sound and prime, filter pressure, heater condition, salt cell output, visible leaks at fittings and unions.

When something is starting to fail, we catch it early, photograph it, and tell you before it becomes an emergency. A pump seal that costs a little to fix today is a burned-out motor next month. Cleaning crews that only handle nets and brushes walk right past those warning signs — we don't.

Every cleaning visit is performed under a licensed pool contractor — which means the person testing your water can also diagnose your pump, heater, or salt system on the same trip. One team, no handoffs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does your weekly pool cleaning include?

Every visit includes skimming, brushing, vacuuming, emptying skimmer and pump baskets, filter cleaning or backwashing as needed, a full water chemistry test and balancing, a salt cell check on salt pools, and an equipment inspection with photo documentation. You receive a service report after each visit.

Do you balance chemicals or just test them?

We balance, not just test. Free chlorine, pH, alkalinity, calcium hardness, cyanuric acid, and salt level where applicable are tested and adjusted on the spot at every visit.

Are your cleaners actually pool technicians?

Yes. Our technicians work under a licensed Florida pool contractor and inspect the equipment pad on every visit — pump, filter, heater, and salt system. If something is starting to fail, we catch it and document it with photos before it becomes an emergency.

What if you find a problem during a cleaning visit?

We photograph it, explain it in your service report, and give you an upfront quote before any repair work begins. Nothing is repaired or billed without your approval.

Is weekly cleaning part of a maintenance plan?

It can be. Weekly cleaning is the foundation of our Pool Maintenance Plans, which add priority scheduling, member pricing on repairs, and seasonal deep-care services like acid washing.
