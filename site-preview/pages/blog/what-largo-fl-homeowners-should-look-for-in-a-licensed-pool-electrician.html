What Largo, FL Homeowners Should Look For in a Licensed Pool Electrician

By Yanni Ramos, Licensed Pool & Electrical Contractor (EC13011994)

A licensed pool electrician in Largo, FL, should hold both a pool contractor license and an electrical contractor license, carry real hands-on experience with pool systems, provide upfront pricing before any work begins, and back the job with a clear written warranty. Anything less puts your equipment, your budget, and your safety at risk. This guide covers each qualification and explains exactly what to verify before you hire.

What Licenses Should a Pool Electrician Hold?

At minimum, a pool electrician should hold a pool contractor license and an electrical contractor license. The pool contractor license proves the company is qualified to work on pool systems specifically. The electrical contractor license confirms they understand the electrical work those systems demand, from bonding and grounding to code-compliant panel connections.

An LP gas license matters too, especially if a gas pool heater or other gas-fired equipment is part of the plan. A general contractor license adds another layer of capability for anything construction-related around the pool.

That combination is rare. Perfect Catch Electric holds all four: CPC1459998 (pool contractor), EC13011994 (electrical contractor), LI4527 (LP gas), and CGC1531306 (general contractor). We are the only contractor in Pinellas County licensed in electrical, pool, and gas under one roof. Every scope of work, from new equipment to swimming pool repair in Largo, FL, is executed under our own licenses with no subcontractors.

How Much Experience Should a Pool Electrician Have?

Look for a contractor with years of hands-on pool electrical work, not general electrical experience applied to a pool for the first time. Pool systems carry their own complexities, and a technician who has spent years on pool equipment understands the details, from wiring and bonding to automation controls.

We have spent six years wiring pools for every major builder in Tampa and Pinellas County. We also build pools under our own pool construction company, so our technicians understand pool equipment at a structural and plumbing level, not just a surface-repair level. That depth is why we can anticipate problems before they surface and handle pool electrical repair and installation correctly the first time.

Code compliance matters as much as experience. Pool electrical work in Largo, FL, must meet local electrical codes to pass inspection and, more importantly, to keep swimmers safe. Every job we perform, including pool wiring and rewiring, is done to code and inspection-ready.

Referrals and reviews round out the picture. A proven track record tells you the work holds up long after the truck leaves. We hold 5-star ratings on Google, Facebook, and Yelp, and much of our work comes from referrals across Largo, from the neighborhoods off Ulmerton Road to homes near the Highland Recreation Complex.

Upfront pricing means you know the full cost before any work begins, which lets you budget accurately and removes the risk of surprise charges midway through the project. It is one of the clearest signals that a contractor runs a professional operation.

Upfront pricing is one of our three guarantees. We agree on the cost before we start, and that number does not change unless you approve additional work first. No hidden fees. No invoices that grow while the job is open. Our shop sits at 13932 Walsingham Rd here in Largo, FL, and our pricing works the same way for every neighbor we serve.

What Should a Pool Electrical Warranty Cover?

A pool electrical warranty should cover both repairs and installations, state its duration clearly, and spell out any exclusions so you know exactly what is protected. If a contractor cannot explain the warranty in plain terms, treat that as a warning sign.

Our best-in-class warranty covers our repairs and installations. If a covered problem returns, we fix it without additional cost or hassle. We stand behind the work because we performed it ourselves, under our own licenses, with no subcontractor in the chain to pass blame to.

How Do You Choose the Right Pool Electrician in Largo, FL?

Choose the contractor who checks every box: multiple licenses, verified pool experience, upfront pricing, a written warranty, and strong reviews. Here is the checklist we give Largo homeowners:

  • Verify licenses, including pool contractor and electrical contractor at minimum
  • Confirm real pool electrical experience, not general electrical work
  • Get pricing in writing before any work begins
  • Ask what the warranty covers and how long it lasts
  • Read reviews and testimonials from past clients
  • Hire a contractor who handles everything in-house

That last point matters more than most homeowners realize. When one company handles the full scope, there is no miscommunication between trades and no split responsibility when something goes wrong. If you are not sure what shape your system is in, a pool system inspection is the right starting point. It tells you exactly what needs attention before any money changes hands.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do pool electricians in Largo, FL need a special license?

Yes. Pool electrical work should be performed by a contractor holding both an electrical contractor license and a pool contractor license. Perfect Catch Electric holds four licenses, including electrical (EC13011994) and pool (CPC1459998), so every part of the job is covered under our own credentials.

How do I verify a pool electrician’s license in Florida?

Florida license numbers can be verified through the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation’s online license lookup. Ask the contractor for their license numbers before hiring; a legitimate company will provide them without hesitation. We publish ours because we want homeowners to check.

Why does it matter if a contractor uses subcontractors?

Subcontractors split responsibility. If a subbed-out portion of the job fails, the companies involved can blame each other while your pool sits unusable. We perform every scope with our own licensed technicians, so accountability stays with one company from the first call to the final inspection.

How often should pool electrical systems be inspected?

Most pool electrical systems benefit from a professional inspection once a year, and any time you notice tripped breakers, flickering pool lights, or equipment shutting off unexpectedly. Largo pools also live with Gulf salt air, which corrodes connections faster than inland conditions and makes periodic inspections even more important.
