Does Your Older Tarpon Springs, FL Home
Need an Electrical Panel Upgrade?

By Yanni Ramos, Licensed Pool & Electrical Contractor (EC13011994)

Most older Tarpon Springs homes do need an electrical panel upgrade, especially houses from the 1960s and 70s still running on their original panels. Flickering lights, warm outlets, frequently tripped breakers, and a growing web of extension cords are the clearest signs a panel can no longer keep up with modern demand. An upgraded panel reduces fire risk, adds capacity for today's appliances, and raises your home's value. Perfect Catch Electric performs panel upgrades throughout Tarpon Springs under our own electrical license, EC13011994, with no subcontractors.

What Are the Signs Your Panel Needs an Upgrade?

Flickering lights and tripped breakers are the two most common signs an electrical panel needs an upgrade. When those show up on a regular basis, the panel is telling you it cannot handle your home's electrical load.

Warm outlets and frequently blown fuses point to the same problem: a system under strain. So does leaning on extension cords and power strips because the home never had enough capacity where you actually need it.

Our electricians serving Tarpon Springs see this constantly in the historic bungalows near downtown and in the neighborhoods along the Anclote River, where homes built in the 1960s and 70s are still running panels sized for a different era. Those panels were installed long before central air, EV chargers, and modern kitchens existed.

An outdated panel is more than an inconvenience. It is a documented fire hazard. And if you are planning to add new appliances or remodel, the panel needs to be evaluated first to confirm it can carry the added load. Upgrading is a practical investment in safety and capacity, not a cosmetic one.

What Are the Benefits of Upgrading?

An upgraded electrical panel gives your home more capacity, better fire protection, improved efficiency, and stronger resale value. Those four benefits cover nearly every reason homeowners call us about panel work.

More capacity means you can power modern appliances and technology without fear of overloads. A new panel also reduces the risk of electrical fires caused by outdated equipment and worn connections, which is the single most important reason to act on the warning signs above.

Efficiency improves as well. A properly sized, properly wired panel helps your systems run the way they were designed to, which supports lower utility costs and a more comfortable home.

Then there is value. In a market like Tarpon Springs, where buyers pay a premium for historic character near the Sponge Docks, an updated panel removes a major inspection red flag and makes the home more attractive at sale. A new panel also brings the home into compliance with current electrical codes.

Panel work is not a handyman job. It should be handled through licensed residential electrical services so every connection meets current safety standards and the work passes inspection the first time.

How Do You Find the Right Contractor for a Panel Upgrade?

The right contractor for a panel upgrade is licensed, experienced specifically with panel replacements, transparent on pricing, and willing to stand behind the work with a real warranty.

Start with licensing. Verify the contractor's credentials before anything else. Perfect Catch Electric holds four licenses, including electrical license EC13011994, and we never hand work to subcontractors. Every job runs under our own name, start to finish. Online reviews and referrals from neighbors are worth checking too.

Next, demand upfront pricing. You should know the full cost before any work starts, with no hidden fees appearing on the final bill. A contractor who cannot give you a clear number before starting is a contractor to avoid. The same standard applies whether you need a full panel replacement or everyday electrical repair work.

Finally, ask about the warranty. A strong warranty on completed work shows the contractor is confident in what they installed, and it protects your investment if anything goes wrong later.

What Happens During a Panel Upgrade?

A panel upgrade starts with a load assessment, moves through permitting and installation, and ends with full circuit testing. Most upgrades are completed in a single day.

Every panel upgrade and replacement we perform begins with an evaluation of your current panel and a conversation about your power needs. That assessment determines the right size and type of panel for your home, including headroom for anything you plan to add later.

From there, we pull the required permits to guarantee compliance with local codes. On installation day, we safely disconnect power, remove the old panel, and install the new one. Once the new panel is in, we reconnect your circuits and run thorough testing to verify everything functions correctly.

Before we leave, we walk you through the new panel and answer your questions. Every job is backed by our warranty, so you are covered after we drive away, not just while we are on site.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my home needs a panel upgrade?

Frequent breaker trips, flickering lights, warm outlets, and heavy reliance on extension cords all point to a panel that needs replacement. Homes in Tarpon Springs, FL, built before 1980 that still have their original panel almost always benefit from a professional evaluation. If you are adding major appliances or remodeling, get the panel assessed before the project starts.

How much does an electrical panel upgrade cost?

Cost depends on the panel size, the condition of your home's wiring, and what your household needs to power. We provide upfront pricing on every job, so you know the full number before any work begins. There are no hidden fees and no surprises on the final bill.

How long does an electrical panel upgrade take?

Most panel upgrades are completed in a single day. Power is disconnected during the work and restored the same day after testing, so disruption to your household stays minimal. Permitting happens before installation day, so the visit itself is efficient.

Can a new panel support an EV charger or new appliances?

Yes, and that is one of the most common reasons homeowners upgrade. A modern panel sized correctly for your home can carry an EV charger, new HVAC equipment, pool equipment, and a modern kitchen without strain. We size every new panel around how you actually plan to use the home, not just its current load.
