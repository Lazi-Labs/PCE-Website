Whole neighborhoods in Town 'n' Country went up within a few years of each other in the 1960s and 1970s, and they are aging on the same schedule. Frequently Asked Questions

Is aluminum branch wiring in my home a problem?

The wire itself is not the issue. The connections are, because aluminum expands and contracts differently than copper and can loosen over time. Remediation addresses the terminations rather than requiring a full rewire.

Do I need a permit for electrical work here?

Town 'n' Country is unincorporated Hillsborough County, which requires permits for service and panel work, new circuits, and generator installations. Device replacement generally does not.

What does pool bonding actually do?

It ties all the metal around the pool — including rails, ladders, the deck reinforcement and the equipment — to the same electrical potential. This prevents current from finding a path through swimmers.

How long does a panel upgrade take?

Most residential panel replacements complete in one day, with power off for a portion of it. Timing depends on utility coordination and inspection scheduling.

Can you add an EV charger to a 1970s home?

Usually yes, though the existing service size determines whether it is a circuit addition or a panel upgrade. We run the load calculation before quoting so you know which project you are looking at.
