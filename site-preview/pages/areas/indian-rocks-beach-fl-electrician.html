Frequently Asked Questions

My insurer flagged my electrical panel. Can you help?

Many Florida insurers are declining renewals on homes with original fuse panels, 60-amp service, or panels with documented failure histories. If your insurer has flagged your panel, we can assess the existing equipment, install a replacement, permit it through the city, and provide the documentation your insurer requires for coverage continuation.

How do I know if my older home needs rewiring?

Homes built before 1973 that have never been evaluated likely have aluminum branch wiring or knob-and-tube remnants. Signs include flickering lights, warm outlets, breakers that trip under light load, or a faint burning smell at the panel. We evaluate the existing system and document what we find before recommending any rewiring scope.

Do you install outdoor lighting circuits in Indian Rocks Beach?

Yes. Outdoor lighting circuits are a standard part of our residential electrical scope in Indian Rocks Beach. All outdoor circuits on the barrier island include GFCI protection and properly sealed weatherproof boxes rated for coastal exposure.
