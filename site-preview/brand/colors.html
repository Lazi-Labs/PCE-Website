Usage

Coral is reserved for the primary conversion action ("Request Service"), text links, and headline offset shadows; its button edge and hover state are Red Deep #A7130C. Navy carries the call-to-action phone button, hero backgrounds, badge bars, and the footer. Teal appears in the logo mark, badge icons, and form-input borders — never as a button fill. Sky/cream/sand are section background washes; body and heading text is #001E33, not black.
